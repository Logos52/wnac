# WNAC — agent contract

Minimal contract; the ecosystem-level rules in `../AGENTS.md` apply (project disambiguation, writing style).

## Doctrine

Method public, money private. No real balances, transactions, or account details ever leave this repo or appear in screenshots — public-facing renders use fake data only.

## Writing style

Decision documents (PRDs, proposals, decision notes, memos) follow **High-Signal Decision Writing** — canonical in `../llm-knowledge-base/02 - System/Writing Standards.md`, digest in `../AGENTS.md`: verdict first, measured claims, steelman the rejected option, price your own recommendation, falsifiable success criteria.

## Reversibility

Confirm before anything destructive or hard to undo. Plan → approve → build → review.


## Feedback protocol (standing rule — canonical in ~/Projects/AGENTS.md)

Deep read before execution, always: enumerate everything Wedge asked, details included, never the gist; find the general principle behind the specific complaint; then execute against both, and record the principle in the appropriate standard. Prose is generated as a continuous explanation to a real person first, then filtered through the writing standards — never assembled from rule-compliant fragments.
